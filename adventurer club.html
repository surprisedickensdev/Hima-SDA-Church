Guiding Principles

Adventurer Pledge & Law

Teaching young hearts the joy of obedience, kindness, and love for Jesus.

The Adventurer Pledge

“Because Jesus loves me,
I will always do my best.”

A simple yet profound commitment reminding our little ones that their motivation for good choices comes from Jesus' unconditional love.

The Adventurer Law

Jesus can help me to:

  • Be obedient
  • Be pure
  • Be true
  • Be kind
  • Be respectful
  • Be attentive
  • Be helpful
  • Be cheerful
  • Be thoughtful
  • Be reverent
Level by Level

Adventurer Achievement Classes & Requirements

Tailored age-appropriate curriculums covering Basic, My God, My Self, My Family, and My World tracks.

Little Lambs

Age 4 / Pre-K

Simple Bible wonders, learning sharing, observing nature, and songs of praise.

Key Requirements:
  • Recite the Adventurer Pledge.
  • Complete the Creation Star or Wooly Lamb award.
  • Learn how to pray simple morning and evening prayers.
  • Practice helping parents with simple chores.
Register Age 4

Early Bird

Age 5 / Kindergarten

Kindergarten discovery: colors, birds, manners, and helping around the home.

Key Requirements:
  • Memorize 2 simple Bible memory verses.
  • Earn the Feathered Friends (Birds) award.
  • Learn the "Please" and "Thank You" courtesy habits.
  • Identify 6 common domestic and wild animals.
Register Age 5

Busy Bee

Age 6 / Grade 1

First grade skills: memorizing the Lord's Prayer, safety rules, and basic health.

Key Requirements:
  • Recite the Adventurer Pledge and Law from memory.
  • Earn the Busy Bee Reading Certificate.
  • Complete the Road Safety or Health Specialist award.
  • Practice table setting and helping during family mealtimes.
Register Age 6

Sunbeam

Age 7 / Grade 2

Being a ray of sunshine to neighbors, road safety, courtesy, and animal friends.

Key Requirements:
  • Read or listen to the story of Jesus' life in the Gospels.
  • Earn the Courtesy Award and Friend of Animals award.
  • Complete a special sunshine visit or gift to someone in need.
  • Learn how to keep emergency numbers and family contacts.
Register Age 7

Builder

Age 8 / Grade 3

Building godly character, money management, media awareness, and Bible books.

Key Requirements:
  • Memorize the names of the 4 Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John).
  • Earn the Media Critic or Temperance award.
  • Practice wise stewardship of pocket money and offerings.
  • Plan and carry out a community service project with family.
Register Age 8

Helping Hand

Age 9 / Grade 4

Preparation for Pathfinders: hygiene, community service, camping intro, and Bible heroes.

Key Requirements:
  • Recite the books of the New Testament in order.
  • Earn the First Aid Helper or Environmentalist award.
  • Lead in a family worship service.
  • Prepare for Pathfinder transition and Investiture ceremony.
Register Age 9
