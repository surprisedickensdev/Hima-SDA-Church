Monthly Program Highlights

What Happens at Saturday AY?

1st Sabbath: Musical Worship & Praise Night
An afternoon dedicated to inspirational music, choir performances, musical testimonies, and congregational singing that lifts the soul.
2nd Sabbath: Bible Bowl & Theological Forums
In-depth discussions answering modern life dilemmas from the Bible. Young people ask questions about science, faith, relationships, and prophecy in a supportive setting.
3rd Sabbath: Outdoor Evangelism & Hospital Ministry
Hands-on outreach: visiting the sick in local Hima clinics, praying with widows and orphans, and sharing Christian literature with our community.
4th Sabbath: Youth Rally & Master Guide Showcase
Joint meeting with Pathfinders, Adventurers, and neighboring youth societies, featuring drills, honors investitures, and spiritual rallies.
