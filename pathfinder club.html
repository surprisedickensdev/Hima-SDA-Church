Mission, Aim, Motto & Pledge

Our Mission

"Save the Lost, Serve Humanity."

The Pathfinder Club operates under the Adventist Youth Ministries, dedicated to winning young hearts to Jesus Christ and leading them into active, unselfish Christian service.

AIM & MOTTO

AIM:

"To take the Advent message to all the world in my Generation."

MOTTO:

"The love of Christ Compels me."

The Pathfinder Pledge

"By the Grace of God,
I will be pure, kind, and true.
I will keep the Pathfinder Law.
I will be a servant of God and a friend to man."

Recited at every Pathfinder induction, flag-raising, and camporee, affirming our lifelong dedication to God and community.

Interactive Law Explorer

The 8 Pathfinder Laws

Click any card below to discover the practical meaning and spiritual application of each law in our daily lives.

Law 1

Keep the Morning Watch

I will pray and study my Bible each day.

Scriptural Insight: Psalm 5:3 — Starting each morning in quiet communion with God provides the spiritual armor and wisdom to conquer every temptation of the day.
Law 2

Do My Honest Part

By the power of God, I will do my share and be dependable.

Scriptural Insight: Ecclesiastes 9:10 — Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with all your might. Fulfilling duties at home, school, and church with integrity.
Law 3

Care for My Body

I will be temperate in all things and strive for physical fitness.

Scriptural Insight: 1 Corinthians 6:19-20 — Our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit. We abstain from harmful substances and maintain active exercise and nutrition.
Law 4

Keep a Level Eye

I will not lie, cheat, or deceive; I will face difficulties bravely.

Scriptural Insight: Proverbs 4:25 — Look straight ahead and fix your eyes on what is before you. Living with moral courage, honesty, and truthfulness.
Law 5

Be Courteous and Obedient

I will be kind to others, respecting parents, teachers, and leaders.

Scriptural Insight: Ephesians 6:1-3 — Cultivating genuine politeness, showing reverence to our elders, and walking in willing obedience.
Law 6

Walk Softly in the Sanctuary

In any place of worship, I will be quiet, reverent, and attentive.

Scriptural Insight: Habakkuk 2:20 — The Lord is in His holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before Him. Maintaining reverence in God's presence.
Law 7

Keep a Song in My Heart

I will be cheerful, praising God even during times of trial.

Scriptural Insight: 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 — Rejoice always; pray without ceasing; give thanks in all circumstances. A cheerful countenance brings light into dark moments.
Law 8

Go on God's Errands

I will always be ready to tell others about Jesus and do good deeds.

Scriptural Insight: Isaiah 6:8 — "Here am I; send me." Eager readiness to serve the needy and share the gospel of Christ's second coming.

Blue Ribbon

Friend Class (Grade 5 / Age 10)

Download Friend Manual (PDF)

The foundational level of the Pathfinder Investiture Achievement curriculum. Friends develop deep devotional habits, learn teamwork, discover outdoor living basics, and practice kindness in the community.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 10 years old or in the 5th Grade.
Memorize and explain the Pathfinder Pledge and Law.
Read the book Steps to Christ or the current junior devotional book.
Maintain an active club attendance record and earn the Reading Certificate.
II. Spiritual Discovery
Memorize the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:3–17) in order.
Study and recite the Model Prayer (Matthew 6:9–13).
Read through the Gospel of Mark and participate in a discussion of Christ's miracles.
Recite the names of the 39 books of the Old Testament.
III. Serving Others
Dedicate at least two hours in community service or environmental cleanup in Hima.
Participate in a visitation program to elderly, sick, or shut-in church members.
Demonstrate good citizenship by helping in your home and church worship setup.
IV. Making Friends
Discover biblical principles of friendship and table manners.
Complete a roleplay activity on standing firm against negative peer pressure.
Demonstrate Christian courtesy, respect for parents, elders, and teachers.
V. Health & Fitness
Complete the Health and Fitness honor or Basic Water Safety honor.
Sign the Pathfinder Christian Temperance Pledge against alcohol, tobacco, and drugs.
Complete the basic physical fitness test (sit-ups, push-ups, shuttle run).
VI. Nature Study
Earn two nature honors (e.g., Mammals, Birds, Trees, Flowers, or Stars).
Go on a one-hour nature walk and identify at least 10 species created by God.
Keep an illustrated nature observation notebook for four weeks.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn the Knot Tying honor: tie and explain 8 basic knots from memory.
Earn Camping Skills I honor (tent pitching, campfire safety, outdoor hygiene).
Hike 3 kilometers (2 miles) in nature with your unit.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Complete one honor in Arts, Crafts, or Household Arts not previously earned.
Participate in an Investiture Service and receive the Friend badge and blue ribbon.

Red Ribbon

Companion Class (Grade 6 / Age 11)

Download Companion Manual (PDF)

Expands outdoor skills, biblical knowledge, and first aid readiness. Companions focus on nature navigation, tree and bird identification, and building spiritual relationships.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 11 years old or in the 6th Grade.
Memorize and recite the Pathfinder Aim, Motto, and Pledge to the Bible.
Daily Bible reading guide (Morning Watch) for at least 8 consecutive weeks.
Read the current Pathfinder Book Club selection.
II. Spiritual Discovery
Memorize all 27 books of the New Testament in sequence.
Study the Parables of Jesus in the Gospels and explain their meaning today.
Memorize Bible Memory Gems on Salvation, Grace, the Sabbath, and the Second Coming.
Understand how the Old Testament Sanctuary service points to Jesus Christ.
III. Serving Others
Plan and conduct a club community outreach project with your unit.
Participate in at least 2 missionary activities (tract distribution, Sabbath School presentation).
Write a note or card of Christian encouragement to at least 3 people.
IV. Making Friends
Practice biblical conflict resolution according to Matthew 18:15–17.
Participate in a group team-building and trust challenge.
Discuss family duties and show practical helpfulness to parents and siblings.
V. Health & Fitness
Earn the First Aid Basic honor (bandaging, wounds, burns, sprains, fainting).
Study the NEWSTART principles (Nutrition, Exercise, Water, Sunlight, Temperance, Air, Rest, Trust).
Complete a 30-day personal physical endurance tracking program.
VI. Nature Study
Earn two nature honors (e.g., Reptiles, Amphibians, Insects, Shells, or Weather).
Identify at least 15 species of birds or trees native to Western Uganda.
Keep an outdoor nature logbook during a club field camp.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn Camping Skills II honor (outdoor cooking, one-match campfire, tool safety for knife and hatchet).
Participate in an overnight club campout.
Complete a 5 km (3-mile) hike carrying personal gear.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Earn one honor in Vocational or Outdoor industries.
Participate in an Investiture Ceremony and receive the Companion badge and red ribbon.

Green Ribbon

Explorer Class (Grade 7 / Age 12)

Download Explorer Manual (PDF)

Focuses on personal discovery, wilderness navigation, astronomy, and Christian history. Explorers lead devotionals, master map reading, and take active roles in church evangelism.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 12 years old or in the 7th Grade.
Active membership in Hima Pathfinder Club for at least one full year.
Complete the Explorer Bible Reading plan through the Gospel accounts and Romans.
Read a selected book on Christian church history or Adventist pioneers.
II. Spiritual Discovery
Study the Protestant Reformation and the history of the Seventh-day Adventist movement.
Understand the significance of 1844 and the Great Disappointment leading to truth.
Memorize 1 Corinthians 13 (The Love Chapter) from memory.
Conduct a unit worship or youth prayer circle.
III. Serving Others
Lead out in a community assistance or welfare program in Hima.
Participate in Voice of Youth evangelistic campaign or church literature outreach.
Organize a food drive or clothing donation for needy children.
IV. Making Friends
Examine the Christian use of time, entertainment, and social media.
Learn active listening skills and empathy for peers facing challenges.
Appreciate cultural diversity and unity in Christ.
V. Health & Fitness
Earn the Basic Rescue honor or Nutrition honor.
Deliver a 3-minute presentation to your unit on the benefits of water and exercise.
Demonstrate knowledge of healthy plant-based meal planning.
VI. Nature Study
Earn the Stars (Astronomy) honor: identify constellations, Orion, planets, and the North Star.
Earn the Weather honor: read barometers, cloud formations, and storm forecasting.
Take part in an ecological tree-planting project.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn Camping Skills III honor: learn square, diagonal, and sheer lashing.
Demonstrate compass reading, finding true north, and topographic map navigation.
Complete an 8 km (5-mile) compass orientation hike.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Complete one honor in Science, Technology, or Craft fields.
Receive the Explorer pin and green ribbon at Investiture.

Silver Ribbon

Ranger Class (Grade 8 / Age 13)

Download Ranger Manual (PDF)

Prepares teens for peer leadership, biblical prophecy defense, advanced first aid (CPR), and wilderness pioneering. Rangers mentor younger units and organize community welfare initiatives.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 13 years old or in the 8th Grade.
Review and teach the Pathfinder Pledge and Law to younger Pathfinders.
Maintain a personal daily devotional journal for at least 8 weeks.
Read selected chapters from Messages to Young People by Ellen G. White.
II. Spiritual Discovery
Study Daniel 2, Daniel 7, and Revelation 14 (The Three Angels' Messages).
Explain the 2300-day prophecy and the Sanctuary doctrine.
Memorize the Beatitudes (Matthew 5:1–12) and present their application.
Lead an evangelistic prayer meeting or Sabbath School program.
III. Serving Others
Spend at least 6 hours in humanitarian community service in the Hima area.
Plan and conduct a church service or hospital visitation initiative.
Assist in teaching drill and marching commands to Friend or Companion units.
IV. Making Friends
Discuss Christian dating principles, modesty, and personal integrity.
Complete a peer mediation exercise for resolving group conflicts.
Demonstrate leadership empathy toward struggling or discouraged youth.
V. Health & Fitness
Earn First Aid Standard honor: learn adult and child CPR techniques.
Demonstrate emergency handling for heatstroke, hypothermia, fractures, and shock.
Conduct a calisthenics or morning fitness warm-up for the club.
VI. Nature Study
Earn two nature honors (e.g., Ferns, Fungi, Ecology, Forestry, or Geology).
Lead a group nature study hike and explain God's design in biodiversity.
Participate in an environmental habitat restoration project.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn Camping Skills IV honor: construct wilderness pioneering shelters without nails.
Demonstrate survival cooking outdoors without pots or modern utensils.
Complete a 10 km (6-mile) hiking expedition with topographical checkpoints.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Earn one honor in Outdoor Industries or Wilderness Living.
Receive the Ranger pin and silver ribbon at Investiture.

Burgundy Ribbon

Voyager Class (Grade 9 / Age 14)

Download Voyager Manual (PDF)

Focuses on advanced discipleship, Christian apologetics, spiritual gifts discovery, and expedition survival. Voyagers serve as apprentice staff officers and develop lifelong leadership habits.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 14 years old or in the 9th Grade.
Write a personal philosophy of Christian living based on Scripture.
Complete a spiritual gifts assessment (Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12).
Read a full Christian biography or missionary classic.
II. Spiritual Discovery
In-depth study of the 28 Fundamental Beliefs of Seventh-day Adventists.
Articulate the doctrine of Righteousness by Faith in Jesus Christ.
Lead a Bible study or Sabbath School lesson discussion for younger youth.
Study Adventist prophetic heritage and the role of Ellen G. White.
III. Serving Others
Direct a community humanitarian initiative or ADRA relief program.
Volunteer at least 8 hours in church community medical or literacy projects.
Mentor an Explorer or Ranger Pathfinder throughout the club training year.
IV. Making Friends
Cultivate emotional intelligence and Christian communication skills.
Examine cross-cultural mission dynamics and community diversity in Uganda.
Lead interactive icebreaker games and team building for junior units.
V. Health & Fitness
Earn the Physical Fitness honor or Nutrition honor.
Plan, cook, and serve a balanced, healthy plant-based meal for your unit.
Lead a youth discussion on mental wellness, stress relief, and prayer.
VI. Nature Study
Complete an Ecology or Environmental Conservation honor.
Investigate local environmental conservation issues in the Rwenzori region.
Organize an outdoor nature worship service for Pathfinders.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn the Wilderness Living honor or Backpacking honor.
Demonstrate day orienteering by compass and night celestial navigation.
Participate in an overnight 15 km (9-mile) expedition campout.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Earn one Master-level or Advanced honor.
Receive the Voyager pin and burgundy ribbon at Investiture.

Gold Ribbon

Guide Class (Grade 10 / Age 15)

Download Guide Manual (PDF)

The pinnacle of the junior Pathfinder curriculum. Guides demonstrate advanced leadership, Christian apologetics defense, wilderness survival mastery, and readiness to transition into Master Guide training.

Complete Achievement Requirements Checklist:

I. Personal Growth
Be 15 years old or in the 10th Grade.
Read The Great Controversy or Desire of Ages selected chapters.
Prepare a comprehensive personal leadership portfolio.
Serve as an assistant unit leader for a junior Pathfinder group.
II. Spiritual Discovery
Christian Apologetics: defend the biblical creation model and authority of Scripture.
Prepare a baptismal candidate or assist in baptismal Bible classes.
Deliver a full sermon or devotional presentation in church or AY program.
Study church organization and Adventist denominational governance.
III. Serving Others
Plan, organize, and execute a major community evangelism outreach.
Lead a youth mission day, health clinic, or orphan care program.
Coordinate an outdoor community prayer walk in Hima.
IV. Making Friends
Master youth leadership psychology, group dynamics, and conflict mediation.
Teach drill and ceremony commands to an entire Pathfinder club assembly.
Demonstrate Christian mentor etiquette and pastoral care for junior youth.
V. Health & Fitness
Earn CPR Certification and Advanced First Aid honor.
Organize and direct a 5 km church health run or fitness expo.
Advocate actively for total abstinence from addictive substances.
VI. Nature Study
Earn two Advanced / Master-level Nature honors.
Instruct younger Pathfinders in completing a nature honor curriculum.
Create an educational nature display, herbarium, or insect taxonomy collection.
VII. Outdoor Living
Earn the Outdoor Leadership honor.
Design and supervise construction of a camp entrance, bridge, or watchtower.
Complete a 20 km (12-mile) survival wilderness hike with overnight bivouac.
VIII. Honor Enrichment
Earn prerequisite honors for the Master Guide leadership curriculum.
Receive the Guide pin and gold ribbon at Investiture; qualify for Master Guide training.

Master Guide (Leadership / Age 16+)

MG Manual (PDF) Portfolio Guide (PDF)

The highest level of leadership training in the Seventh-day Adventist youth ministry. Master Guides are trained directors and leaders equipped to mentor Adventurers, Pathfinders, and Senior Youth.

Complete Master Guide Leadership Requirements:

I. Prerequisites
Be a baptized member of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
Be at least 16 years of age at registration.
Hold a current recommendation from the Hima SDA Church Board.
Complete the 10-hour Basic Staff Training (BST) seminar.
II. Spiritual Development
Read the books Steps to Christ and Education by Ellen G. White.
Complete a one-year personal Bible reading plan through the entire Scriptures.
Write a comprehensive essay on your personal spiritual growth and faith journey.
Teach a biblical baptismal or foundational doctrine class to youth.
III. Skills Development
Earn at least 2 honors from Nature, 2 from Outdoor Industries, and 1 from Health & Science.
Hold valid certification in First Aid & CPR from a recognized authority.
Supervise a club drill and ceremonial flag presentation.
IV. Child & Youth Development
Attend seminars on child psychology, adolescent behavior, and positive mentoring.
Complete mandatory Child Protection and Safeguarding training.
Present a workshop on communication with children or teenagers.
V. Leadership Field Work
Serve as an active staff leader in Adventurers or Pathfinders for one full year.
Teach at least two honors to completion and lead candidates to investiture.
Attend a Conference/Field Camporee or Leadership Convention.
VI. Outdoor Living & Portfolio
Complete a 25 km (15-mile) outdoor survival expedition or campout.
Compile and present the official Master Guide Portfolio to the Conference Youth Director.
Pass the final oral and practical field examination for investiture.
