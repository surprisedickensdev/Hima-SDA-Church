Senior Youth Development

The Four Ambassador Pillars

Bridging the gap between Pathfinders and adult church life with purpose and fellowship.

Spiritual Life

Deep Bible study, developing a personal worldview grounded in Scripture, and prayer partnerships.

Vocational Skills

Career guidance, resume writing, entrepreneurial workshops, and vocational mentorship.

Community Mission

Leading impactful social projects, health outreach, and youth-to-youth evangelism in Hima.

Outdoor Leadership

Wilderness survival expeditions, Master Guide mentoring, team building, and challenge courses.
