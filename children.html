Age Divisions

Nurturing Young Disciples

Every child in Hima SDA Church is welcomed into an age-appropriate class filled with loving teachers, songs, and Bible lessons.

Cradle Roll

Ages 0 to 2

Sensory Bible songs, baby dedication support, and creating a tender environment of Christian love.

Kindergarten

Ages 3 to 5

Bible story dramatizations, memory verse songs, coloring sheets, and learning about Jesus as Creator.

Primary Class

Ages 6 to 9

Reading Scripture independently, mission spotlight videos, learning the Ten Commandments, and Christian values.

Junior Class

Ages 10 to 12

Deeper Bible exploration, interactive discussion, prayer partners, and preparing for Pathfinder investitures.

Special Highlights

Vacation Bible School (VBS) & Choir

Annual Vacation Bible School (VBS)

Every school holiday, Hima SDA Church hosts a week-long community Vacation Bible School. Over 100 children from Hima gather for morning songs, creative science and crafts, puppet shows, Bible discoveries, and nutritious snacks.

Children's Praise Choir

Our children's choir ministers regularly during Sabbath Divine Services. Children learn vocal harmony, stage confidence, and worship etiquette while offering their voices in praise to God.
