Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions for first-time visitors, parents, and interested community members.

What should I expect when visiting Hima SDA Church on Sabbath?
You will be greeted warmly! Our services start at 9:00 AM on Saturday with Sabbath School (interactive Bible study classes grouped by age). At 11:00 AM, we join together for Divine Worship featuring joyful praise singing, prayer, and a practical biblical sermon. Visitors are always welcome, and there is no pressure to participate beyond what you feel comfortable with.
Can my child join Pathfinder or Adventurer Club if we are not Adventists?
Yes, absolutely! Both Pathfinder (ages 10-15) and Adventurer (ages 4-9) clubs are open to all community children and youth, regardless of religious background. Our clubs focus on building positive moral character, life skills, outdoor appreciation, and loving respect for God and mankind.
Why do Seventh-day Adventists worship on Saturday instead of Sunday?
Seventh-day Adventists observe the biblical Sabbath from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday in harmony with the Fourth Commandment (Exodus 20:8-11), Jesus' own practice (Luke 4:16), and the biblical record of Creation (Genesis 2:1-3). The Sabbath is a sacred memorial of God's creative and redeeming love.
What is the dress code for church services?
We encourage modest, neat, and respectful attire that honors God. Most members wear smart casual or traditional African church wear, and Pathfinders wear their crisp uniforms on designated club Sabbaths. Come as you are — the heart is what matters most to God!
How can I prepare for baptism or request Bible studies?
We offer free, personalized Bible study classes either in person or with study guides. When a person makes a personal decision to accept Jesus Christ as their Lord and Savior and follow His Word, baptism by immersion is celebrated joyfully. Contact our pastoral team through the Staff & Contact page to begin.
