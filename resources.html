Elder's Handbook: Roles and Responsibilities

Spiritual Leadership: The local church elder is called to be a spiritual shepherd alongside the district pastor. Key responsibilities include:

  • Leading meaningful, Christ-centered Sabbath worship and preaching sound doctrine.
  • Conducting communion services (Ordinance of Humility and Lord's Supper) with reverence.
  • Faithful pastoral care: visiting members, the sick, and encouraging backsliders.
  • Presiding over church business sessions in harmony with the Church Manual.

Church Manual: Core Governance & Ordinances

Why a Church Manual? God is a God of order (1 Corinthians 14:40). The Church Manual exists to protect biblical truth, establish fair democratic church processes, and ensure that every local church operates in harmony with the global body of believers.

Key chapters include Membership qualifications, Officers and their duties, Church Board authority, Finance & Tithes, and Marriage and Divorce guidelines.

  • October: Pastor Appreciation Day & Creation Sabbath.
  • Quarterly: 13th Sabbath Mission Projects (East-Central Africa Division).
