Adventist Women's Ministries (AWM)

Nurturing women of all ages, empowering them in spiritual leadership, and ministering to families and the community with Christlike love.

Home / Ministries / Women's Ministries
Our Threefold Purpose

Nurture, Empower & Outreach

Nurture

Deepening personal prayer life, Bible study, and building an uplifting sisterhood of mutual encouragement.

Empower

Equipping women with leadership training, parenting skills, home economics, financial stewardship, and health education.

Outreach

Visiting the sick, supporting single mothers and widows, prison ministries, and community evangelism in Hima.

Annual Calendar Highlights

AWM Church & Community Events

March

International Women's Day of Prayer

A worldwide Sabbath where women lead the entire church service in intercession for families, church leaders, and nations.

August

enditnow® Emphasis Day

Raising community awareness and standing decisively against domestic abuse, gender-based violence, and neglect in society.

Monthly

Women's Prayer Breakfast

Monthly Sunday morning fellowship where women share breakfast, personal testimonies, and intercessory prayer for our youth.

Annual

Spiritual Retreat & Health Expo

An uplifting spiritual getaway featuring health seminars, natural remedies, nutritional demonstrations, and quiet devotional hours.
